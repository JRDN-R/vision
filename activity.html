First time? Enable activity access on the PC.

Run this once in Windows PowerShell as Administrator on the Vision PC, while no jobs are processing. Enter the Google email you use for Vision and confirm access. This keeps your existing projects, models, and text logs.

$p="$env:TEMP\Enable-Vision-Activity.ps1"; Invoke-WebRequest -UseBasicParsing 'https://raw.githubusercontent.com/JRDN-R/vision/main/vision-pc/Enable-Vision-Activity.ps1' -OutFile $p; & powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File $p

Then open this page on your phone or computer. The PC and its existing Tailscale connection must remain online. No installation is needed on the viewing device.
